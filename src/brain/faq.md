## When are salad days?

Salad days are Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Every plate is made fresh on the day of fulfillment.

## When do I need to order by?

The cutoff is 8:00 AM Trinidad time on your fulfillment day. Same-day orders on Monday, Wednesday or Friday are open until 8:00 AM. After that, please choose the next salad day.

## Can I order for the same day?

Yes, if you get your order in before 8:00 AM Trinidad time on a Monday, Wednesday or Friday. After 8:00 AM, your order rolls to the next salad day.

## When does delivery start?

Delivery 11 AM – about 2 PM along the east west corridor.

## How much is delivery?

Curepe to Arima is $30. Trincity and environs is $20. Cathy's team confirms the final fee when they review your order.

## Where can I pick up my order?

Pickup is at Trincity, 2 PM to 5 PM, or St. Clair, 11 AM to 1 PM on your fulfillment day.

## What about prices?

Salads are $45 each (minimum 5 items for delivery). Healthy meals are $50 each (minimum 5 items for delivery). Beef, fish or shrimp add $5 per item. Cold-pressed juices are $18 each (minimum 6 drinks for delivery). Ginger shots are $8 each (minimum 6 drinks for delivery). Cathy's team confirms your final total, including delivery, after you submit your order.

## Which proteins can I choose?

Chicken, beef, fish, shrimp or vegetarian on salads and healthy meals. Beef, fish and shrimp add $5 per item. Choose the protein on the product card and the price updates right away.

## Are your juices suitable for diabetics?

Our cold-pressed juices and ginger shots are diabetic-friendly: made with no added sugar from specially sourced produce. Fruit and vegetables still contain natural sugars, so if you manage diabetes, please check portions with your doctor.

## What do vegetarian options include?

Vegetarian options contain no meat or seafood but may include dairy (such as feta) and egg.

## How do the weekly menus work?

Healthy meals alternate between Menu A and Menu B each week. Choose your delivery date first to see the meals for that week. Salads and juices do not rotate.

## What are the order minimums?

For delivery, food orders need at least 5 items in total. Salads and healthy meals can be combined. Drink orders for delivery need at least 6 items in total. Juices and ginger shots can be combined. Pickup orders have no minimum.

## How far ahead can I order?

You can book any Monday, Wednesday or Friday up to six months ahead. Choose your date before adding meals. The menu matches your selected week.

## Can I set up a recurring order?

Yes. At checkout you can ask us to repeat this order weekly or monthly. You are only billed for the order you are placing now, and we contact you before each repeat.

## How do I confirm my order?

Place your order through the website. After you submit, you'll see a confirmation screen with your order reference and full details. Cathy's team then reviews it and follows up to confirm availability, fulfillment details and payment.

## Do I need an account?

No, an account is completely optional. You can check out as a guest any time. Creating an account simply saves your details for faster future orders and keeps your order history in one place.
