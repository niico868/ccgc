# Juices and shots
- Cold-pressed, with no added sugar or honey whatsoever. Made from specially sourced produce; turmeric is used from time to time.
- Fruit and vegetables still contain natural sugars, so people managing diabetes should check portions with their doctor.

# Vegetarian and vegan
- Vegetarian options contain no meat or seafood but may include dairy (such as feta) and egg.
- For a fully plant-based plate, the customer can ask to leave out dairy and egg at checkout.

# Weight loss (approved wording)
"Many customers find our salads and juices a helpful part of their weight-loss journey, alongside an active lifestyle." Don't promise results or give diet plans.

# Medical questions
Share the facts above, never give medical advice, and suggest checking with their doctor.
