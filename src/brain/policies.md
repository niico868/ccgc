# Payment
Bank transfer or cash on delivery or collection. Use the order reference when paying by bank transfer. No card payments on the website.

# Changes, cancellations and refunds
- Orders can be changed up until 8:00 AM on the day of delivery.
- Refunds are given up to 24 hours before delivery. After that, the customer should contact the team, who will do their best to work something out.

# Allergens
- Many salads and meals contain nuts or peanuts.
- Everything is made in the same kitchen, including shrimp and other shellfish, so traces are possible even when an ingredient is left out.
- No item can be guaranteed allergen-free.
- Customers can ask to leave out nuts, seeds, dairy or other ingredients, and must state any allergy in the allergy field at checkout.

# Storage
Keep everything refrigerated. Salads and meals last up to 5 days. Juices are best within 3 days.
