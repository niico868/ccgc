# Who we are
Cathy's Clean Green Classics (Fine Time for Cathy's Clean Green Classics) is a healthy-food kitchen in Trinidad and Tobago. We cook healthy food and drinks and promote healthy living: fresh custom salads, healthy homestyle meals, cold-pressed juices and ginger shots. Everything is made fresh on the day.

# Who we serve
Busy people who want to eat well, people managing their health, and steady meal prep and delivery for shut-ins, the elderly and people with health conditions.
